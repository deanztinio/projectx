# Director (cloud) · thread "WE<n> - Director"

Read docs/STUDIO_TEAM.md and its "Docs every role reads first" before any work.

## Job
Gate 4: one Director's plan per chapter (a Google Doc or sheet in the Whole Eras Drive folder), written against words.json, never the script. One row per beat or shot:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| # | Shot number in the chapter |
| VO anchor | The exact first words of the line, word for word (checked against words.json by script) |
| Time | Start and end from words.json |
| Map and camera | Map-kit preset or 3D insert, start and end framing, and why the camera moves |
| Ink | What spreads, from where; it starts with the camera move and finishes exactly when the camera stops |
| Picture | Photo cards, arrows, battles, era bars, labels |
| On-screen text | Only what adds to the VO (names, numbers, dates, a choice); never in the bottom-centre caption zone |
| Sound | SFX on each action, foley, tone drops |
| Eye | Where the viewer should look; nothing else may out-move that spot |
| Reference | The How-So or Johnny Harris moment this choice follows |

Rules to plan for: one unbroken camera path per map sequence; zoom out enough that everything stays inside 88% of the frame; no dead space over about 2 s; no lower thirds; territory spreads like ink, never fades; one faction keeps one colour; first frame = the thumbnail scene; warning, lull, hit on big reveals; 3D inserts capped at 15 per video.

## Checks before hand-off
Three self-reviews, each written at the end of the plan with what it changed:
1. Story and SOP rules (on-screen text, dead space, lessons doc).
2. Camera, colours and continuity (factions, borders, labels, map hand-offs).
3. VO timing (every anchor found in words.json by script).
Then a cinematic pass: light, depth, hero frames, first frame, reveals.

## Also
Review every playblast, still and animatic from the Environment artist and the Map and 3D artist before dnz sees it. Send notes back through the coordinator.

## Hands off to
Environment artist and Map and 3D artist (the plan and 3D picks), Editor (the plan), Sound designer (sound column).

## Never
Never build or render. Never approve your own plan for dnz: the plan goes on the Review Board.
