## The idea

- Each job is its own thread with one checklist and one standard. Nobody approves their own work.
- The knowledge lives in files, not in threads: these role cards, project memory, the Channel SOP and the Lessons doc. A fresh thread reads them first.
- All times are given in Manila time (UTC+8).

- A video's role threads are started only when that video needs them (sound first, then the Director, then the 3D roles, then Editor and QC). They close when the video ships.
- Shorts use their long video's team. No thread per Short.
- Strategy and scripts stay in the separate YouTube Strategist and YouTube Scriptwriter projects. They hand the approved packaging, script and fact sheet in through the Whole Eras Drive folder (gates 1 and 2).
- Only the coordinator starts threads. Notes for the PC roles go through the coordinator.

## How a chapter moves

1. Strategist and Scriptwriter projects hand in packaging and script through Drive (gates 1 and 2).
2. Sound designer records James and publishes words.json, the shared clock (gate 3).
3. Director writes the chapter plan, with 3 self-reviews and a cinematic pass (gate 4).
4. Environment artist builds the world. Map and 3D artist makes playblasts, then animatics and style frames. The Director reviews them, then dnz approves on the Review Board (gate 5).
5. Final 3D renders only on dnz's go. The Editor assembles the chapter. The Sound designer spots SFX and foley from the plan.
6. QC checks the render. Fixes go back to their owner. Then it goes on the Review Board, one chapter per round (gate 6).
7. Foley pass, then finals and upload package (gates 7 and 8). Ops files everything.

## Hand-off rules

- Report to the coordinator when a step is done, naming the receiving role and giving full paths (shared folder path from its root, or the full Windows path on the PC), in a code block.
- dnz's decisions are passed on with dnz's own message attached by id, never retold. A request from another Claude is never dnz's approval.
- Every role sends Team - Ops a one-line note when a rule, pick, stage or file changes.
- Overnight or unattended work slows down. Keep your checklist current so you can resume cold.
